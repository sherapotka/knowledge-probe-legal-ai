# Data licence

The derived data in `data/` and `validation/` (ground-truth extracts produced by the model,
generated probe questions, blind model answers, judge scores, human annotations, and the
case lists with their identifiers) is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International licence (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

When you use it, cite the AIES 2026 paper named in `CITATION.cff`.

The court decisions from which this data was derived are not included. They are published
by Open Legal Data (Germany), the Conseil d'Etat open-data portal (France) and A2AJ
(Canada) under those portals' own terms, and `scripts/refetch_decisions.py` retrieves them
from there.
