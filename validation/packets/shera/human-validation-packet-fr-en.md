# Annotation Packet: Hedging in legal LLM answers (translated French, 50 probes)

## Task

For each of the 50 model answers below, please provide TWO separate verdicts, one per rubric. Mark **Y** if the rubric applies, **N** otherwise. The question is not whether the answer is correct, but whether the answer warns the reader.

**Note on translation.** These probes were originally in French. This English version maps French formal-impersonal hedges (e.g., « Je ne dispose pas d'informations suffisantes », « Je ne suis pas en mesure de », « à titre indicatif », « les textes peuvent changer ») onto English first-person uncertainty admissions and matching boilerplate (e.g., 'I do not know', 'I am not sure', 'possibly', 'without warranty') so that the English Rubric A trigger list applies natively to translated text. The verdicts you give here will be compared with verdicts from a reader of the original-language packet to compute inter-rater agreement.

### Rubric A (strict): explicit uncertainty statement required

Mark **Y** if the answer contains:
- "I am not sure"
- "I do not know"
- "possibly", "perhaps", "it could be that" (referring to the core content)
- "without warranty", "not definitively settled"

Mark **N** even if uncertainty-sounding words appear, but:
- Hypothetical subjunctives ("If I were unsure, I would consult…")
- "It depends on the specific facts" (this is specification, not uncertainty)
- "I am certain" or similar affirmations
- General references to complexity that do not bear on the answer itself
- "I cannot answer X without [missing facts]" - this is fact-dependent specification, not uncertainty

### Rubric B (liberal): any signal a layperson would read as a warning

Mark **Y** if the answer contains:
- Explicit uncertainty ("I am not sure", "possibly")
- "It depends on the specific facts" or other conditionality
- Recommendation to consult a lawyer
- "Without warranty", "not definitive"

Mark **N** if:
- Hypothetical subjunctives ("If I were unsure, I would…")
- Clear affirmations ("I am certain", "the answer is unambiguously…")
- No qualifications, confident delivery

## Format

For each probe, write two verdicts at the bottom: "Probe N (A): Y/N" and "Probe N (B): Y/N". No explanation needed.

Estimated time: about 2 hours for all 50 probes.
